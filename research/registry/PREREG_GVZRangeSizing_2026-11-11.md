# Range forecasts for benchmark sizing

Predict log daily UTC XAUUSD high-low range as a fraction of the opening bid. Score weekdays with at least 75 M15 observations and positive range. Forecast before the UTC day starts, regardless of eventual coverage. Price-only features: logs of the last valid range and trailing five- and 22-observation mean ranges. Add one feature: log of the most recent strictly prior GVZ close, at most four calendar days old. Fit ordinary least squares once per year on common valid dates strictly before January 1; minimum 252 observations. Score 2012–2015, 2016–2020, 2021–2025 by paired absolute log error. Secondary diagnostic: half the archived benchmark lot size on dates with predicted log range above the training sample's 80th percentile, if 0.01 lots remains feasible. Compare baseline, price-only and augmented policies on identical trades; no refitting exits, gates or portfolio state. Separately identify archival windows with different account rules. No later dates, hyperparameter search or promotion from the sizing diagnostic.
