# XAUUSD quote archive data quality and market profile

Summarize every obtainable January–October 2026 Exness Standard XAUUSDm monthly file, solely as a market-data handoff. Preserve the original ZIP. Derive UTC minute bid and ask OHLC and quote count, plus mean/min/max quoted spread from quote updates. Exclude invalid timestamps, symbols, nonpositive/nonfinite/crossed quotes and count exclusions. Preserve source order for tied milliseconds. Report timestamps out of order. Do not forward-fill missing minutes, infer fills, join another feed or include November observations.

Describe liquidity across four predetermined, nonoverlapping local clock groups during weekdays. London business hours are 08:00–17:00 Europe/London. New York business hours are 08:00–17:00 America/New_York. Groups are London only, New York only, overlap and outside both. Use weekdays on each location's clock in defining that location's active hours. Report minute counts and medians of quote updates per observed minute, intraminute bid range, and within-minute mean quoted spread. These are descriptive, observed-minute-weighted quantities in USD/oz for the price measures. No trade volume or order-fill interpretation. Do not select a preferred hour, fit a model, or evaluate any trade. Successful handoff requires source-row accounting, correct side OHLC, independent spot checks against source rows and explicit coverage limitations.
