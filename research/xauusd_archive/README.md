# XAUUSD market research archive

The [file-by-file index](FILE_INDEX.md) links individual months, candle arrays, economic series, vintage workbooks, research papers and the original project inputs.

This folder is the local handoff for the gold market research phase. Open [verification.json](verification.json) for the measured footprint, monthly availability and independent minute checks. [The market atlas](../GOLD_MARKET_ATLAS.md) contains the findings. The price and reference data are stored on this laptop under `payload/`. Git tracks the catalogs, methods and results. To transfer the archive to another computer, copy this entire folder, including `payload/`, and retain the project code if you want to reproduce the processing.

## Reading the market correctly

Local copies of accessible papers, documentation and venue reference pages are indexed in the [source library](literature_catalog.json). PDFs preserve the returned document. Text files contain the extracted page text. The catalog records retrieval failures.

**Prices describe specific venues.** XAUUSD in this project is a broker quote in USD per troy ounce. A Standard account and a Raw Spread account can show different prices at the same instant. In London, separate auctions beginning at 10:30 and 15:00 local time establish benchmarks for unallocated gold. Shanghai's afternoon auction starts at 14:15 local time and can take several rounds. Comparing prices across these mechanisms requires matching currency, unit, time and instrument. [Exness tick history](https://www.exness.com/tick-history/) · [LBMA benchmarks](https://www.lbma.org.uk/prices-and-data/lbma-precious-metal-prices) · [Shanghai contract specification](https://en.sge.com.cn/upload/file/201703/24/RGDmtsfPVEzy935O.pdf)

**The quoted side matters.** A buyer crosses the ask and liquidates at the bid. A short sale enters at the bid and covers at the ask. Closing-price movements smaller than the round-trip friction can disappear even when their average direction is statistically measurable. The original extreme M1 and M15 reversal studies measured $0.019/oz and $0.053/oz respectively, against modeled average hurdles around $0.33/oz. Quote counts measure updates from that feed. Fills, available size and exchange aggressor flow require different records. [Economic measurements](../MARKET_EDGE_DISCOVERY.md)

**Typical movement changes with time and conditions.** Absolute M15 returns from the preceding hour improved the historical fixed-mean forecast of next-bar movement size in three later periods. Reported squared-error reductions ranged from 12.4% to 23.6%. The direction model failed its registered criterion. A separate six-timeframe study quantified partial autocorrelation, nonlinear dependence and historical changes in movement size. Both studies condition on continuous observed bars. Gap risk and execution costs require separate measurements. [Forecast study](../registry/RESULT_GoldM15Forecast_2026-11-18.md) · [Market structure](../registry/RESULT_GoldMarketStructure_2026-11-18.md)

**Tails matter for exposure.** Historical excess kurtosis reached 74.36 on M1 and 41.48 on M15. A normal approximation gives too little weight to very large observed moves. In the separate barrier study, 1,170 of 27,110 eligible four-hour paths had ambiguous barrier ordering at the study's available resolution. Archived tick data can resolve order within the corresponding venue and covered dates. [Distribution atlas](../GOLD_MARKET_ATLAS.md) · [Barrier measurements](../results/finalization_2026-11-18/path_risk.json)

**A macro explanation can lose most of its strength one observation later.** In the project's daily proxy panel, the contemporaneous gold–dollar correlation was −0.397 and the one-row-lag relationship was −0.048. The corresponding 10-year yield values were −0.163 and −0.043. The proxy series have different close and release conventions. Interpret these as sample associations and preserve the true information time before calculating a forecast. [Market atlas and data limitations](../GOLD_MARKET_ATLAS.md)

**Know when information became available.** A CFTC Tuesday position normally becomes public on Friday, with exceptions. A revised CPI series shows a later version of an earlier month. The Philadelphia Fed workbooks let a researcher select a historical vintage. Consensus expectations, exact release times and the source's availability to the trader need independent evidence. A change in official gold reserves is a change in reported stocks and does not identify the date of a market purchase. [CFTC release calendar](https://www.cftc.gov/MarketReports/CommitmentsofTraders/ReleaseSchedule/index.htm) · [Philadelphia Fed CPI vintages](https://www.philadelphiafed.org/surveys-and-data/real-time-data-research/pcpi)

## Using and verifying the files

Open an array with `numpy.load(path, mmap_mode="r", allow_pickle=False)`. In the derived bid/ask minutes, `time` is the UTC Unix second when the minute opened. OHLC is in USD/oz. The mean spread gives equal weight to each quoted update within the minute. Each recorded row represents an observed minute. Tied quote timestamps retain feed order. A missing row identifies a period without observations in the file; use the relevant historical venue calendar to distinguish closures from missing data.

Run `python -m scripts.validation.gold_archive_verify` from the project root to rehash the catalogued payloads and check a complete 2026 minute against its original quotes in each available month. The original master inputs, independently downloaded broker files and derived minute bars retain separate provenance. Historical studies continue to use their original data versions.
