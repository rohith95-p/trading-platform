Require positive volume and >=10 trades, then absolute imbalance above the previous 60 eligible sessions' 80th percentile. Follow the sign on XAUUSD at 14:45 London, exit at 15:15 London. Fixed 0.02 lots, executable sides and realistic ECN costs. No intraday barrier at screening stage. Start data in 2021; score 2022–2023 and 2024–2025 separately.
