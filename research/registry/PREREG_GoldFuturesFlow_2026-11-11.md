# COMEX gold flow and subsequent XAUUSD returns

Candidate for research only. Once each London weekday, use 14:00–14:30 Europe/London trades on the outright GC contract with the highest outright volume in the previous completed trading session. Exclude spreads, corrections and unclassified trades. Define imbalance as buyer-initiated volume minus seller-initiated volume, divided by classified volume. Skip if the source cannot identify the aggressor, if there is a feed gap or if less than 95% of regular volume has a known side. No proxy from candle color.

Follow the sign when the absolute imbalance exceeds the trailing 60 eligible sessions' 80th percentile. Require 60 prior observations. Enter on the first executable XAUUSD quote at or after 14:31 London, outside restricted news windows, with a one-minute maximum wait. Stop at one prior 30-minute XAUUSD high-low range from entry. Exit at the first executable quote at or after 15:00 London unless stopped sooner. No take-profit or pyramiding. Do not trade a feed-delay discrepancy. Model the actual side of the spread, slippage, fees and any rule deductions.

Compare with random direction on exactly the same opportunities and with following the preceding 30-minute XAUUSD move on those opportunities. Primary endpoint is incremental net expectancy against both controls. Use calendar-aligned returns, block uncertainty, observed win counts, PF, drawdowns, Sortino, Omega, Calmar, recovery, expected maximum SR and DSR sensitivity across documented trials, multiplicity correction, path reshuffles and tail ablation. Report missing data and losses. Require net PF >=1.30 in each chronological evaluation era, target pooled >=1.40, and positive multiplicity-adjusted evidence for incremental expectancy. Prior studies count in the research history.

Before testing, lock contract-roll implementation, feed coverage, account terms and data hashes. Reserve prospective sessions after specification freeze. If historical screening passes, run evaluation then funded with cash withdrawals, rule breaches, news/hold deductions, financed costs and activation fees. Evaluate first and second net $100 cash payouts and time to each. No automatic promotion on a historical pass.
