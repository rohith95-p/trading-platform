# Delayed dollar and nominal-yield agreement on spot gold

Motivation: the exploratory PathRisk study on the daily proxy shows different following-row gold returns after joint USD and nominal yield moves. Those closes can be asynchronous. Test transport to actual spot gold with conservative delay and explicit executable clock endpoints. No external surprise/consensus feed is available.

For each New York spot session D with both the 09:45 and 15:45 open, find the second most recent distinct dated macro observation strictly before D, calculated from the d1_study/master.csv closes. No same-day data. Skip if D minus feature date exceeds seven calendar days or prior change spans over five calendar days. When log DXY and change in y10 are both strictly negative propose long, when both positive propose short. Abstain otherwise. No magnitude threshold or tuning. Exclude days without a continuous grid of all 25 M15 candles from 09:45 through 15:45. Stage 1 is a distributional feasibility test at observed bid opens, entry and exit only. Reconstruct spread on the required side, charge two ECN slips and RT commission. Compare per event to always-long, always-short and balanced long/short at the exact same endpoints. Report all three eras 2005–2013, 2014–2020, 2021–October 2026. Exclude November holdout.

Stop before strategy construction unless each era has >=100 observations, positive estimated net move per ounce and positive advantage over always-long. Require pooled monthly cluster-bootstrap 95% lower bound on advantage versus long >0, 1999 draws fixed seed 81. Report side frequencies, effect sizes and ordinary/doubled cost results. No formal independent confirmation—all eras already explored. If eligible, implement as an experimental BacktestEngine candidate with a separate registered stop, timeout, sizing and Atlas account evaluation. A failed cheap economic test ends this family without inventing a PF or payout probability. Raw proxy vintage risk and margin/paths remain outside this stage.
