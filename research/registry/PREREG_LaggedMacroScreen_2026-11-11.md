# Exploratory lagged macro screen

Use the existing daily gold proxy, DXY and nominal ten-year yield through 2025. At each close use the previous completed session's changes versus five sessions earlier. When dollar and yield changes agree, predict the opposite direction for gold over the next close-to-close interval. Test that rule and one fixed variant which acts only when the preceding five-session gold move disagrees with the predicted direction. Compare with holding the gold proxy. Charge 2 basis points per unit of position change, including entry and final liquidation. Report results before 2012, 2012–2018 and 2019–2025. All history is exploratory. Require positive net returns in each era and improvement over a same-timing reversed-direction control in each before considering further research. No variants added after running.
