## Mechanism and fixed comparisons

Earlier master-series discovery found small next-bar reversal after extreme M1/M15 returns, below modeled costs. Replicate the same extreme definition on the independently sourced saved Dukascopy bid/ask minute bars. The hypothesis is that the reference-price reversal either survives causal quote-side marking or disappears as microstructure/friction.

1. Input only `research/data/dukascopy_2025/XAUUSD_M1_bidask.npy`, expected SHA256 `71b2a48870e0b53bf8e634fb3ebc8589dd88d44a828559e618d2b0dd104328f8`. December2024 data is warmup only; outcomes restricted to 2025. Fail on duplicate/non-increasing/off-minute timestamps, invalid OHLC/nonpositive/crossed quotes. No data download or interpolation.
2. Construct mid OHLC as the arithmetic mean of the corresponding bid/ask fields. M15 uses complete UTC-aligned groups of15 exact consecutive minute observations, with first open and last close. The mean of separate bid/ask extrema is a descriptive proxy, not an observed synchronous mid high/low; no barrier/path conclusion uses it.
3. Compute exact-adjacent close log returns. Volatility is the RMS of the previous60 valid adjacent returns, excluding the current return. Extreme is `abs(return)>2*lagged_RMS`. No threshold sweep; M1 and M15 only.
4. Trigger after the signal interval is complete. Event direction is the negative sign of its return. Outcome begins at the next interval's first observed quote open and ends at the following interval's first quote open (holding exactly one resolution). All intervening M1 observations must be present and both boundary quotes exact. Compare the original close-reference signed next-close movement with causal mid-open movement and actual buy-Ask/sell-Bid or sell-Bid/buy-Ask outcome. Quotes are separate sampled minute sides, not certified simultaneous ticks or guaranteed broker fills. Report rather than hide open/close reference differences.
5. Descriptive costs per ounce: observed endpoint half-spread sum at1x/1.5x/2x; fixed commission0.035/oz; added adverse slippage0/0.04/0.10 per round trip. All nine combinations reported. No financing, stop, risk allocation, synthetic fill path, PF, account equity or payout result is manufactured. Outcomes may overlap; they are event observations, not independently attainable positions.
6. Controls: (a) balanced random direction at the same timestamps, calculated exactly as the mean of long and short quote-side outcomes; (b) prior ordinary-return observations matched on same UTC month, UTC clock hour and prior-return sign, with lagged RMS between half and twice the extreme's RMS. Use the latest of at most20 preceding ordinary observations in that group within7calendar days. Reuse permitted and counted. Match by information available at the extreme's decision timestamp; never choose controls using subsequent outcome. Ordinary controls use the identical reversal direction convention and executable boundary rules.
7. Four primary tests: M1/M15 mean quote-side outcome minus fixed commission0.035 and slippage0.04, and matched extreme-minus-ordinary difference at those costs. UTC-week cluster bootstrap1999 draws, seed20261118, percentile95% intervals, centered two-sided p, Bonferroni4. Bootstrap matched differences by extreme-event week; disclose reused controls and resulting residual dependence. Monthly means and event counts are descriptive stability checks, no extra significance searches.

## Interpretation and failure conditions

No promotion if the default-cost outcome is nonpositive, if uncertainty includes zero, if matching lacks adequate events/weeks, or if a result depends on one month. Even positive results would remain exploratory mechanism evidence requiring separately committed BacktestEngine strategy tests and genuine prospective confirmation. Finite2025 source coverage, quote synchronization, tick-crosscheck failure and unknown Atlas pricing remain explicit limitations. No stop/target optimization or new directional family is permitted by this protocol.

## Disclosed matched-control correction after first computation

The first computation found negative absolute quote-side outcomes at both resolutions. Inspection of its unusually large positive extreme-minus-ordinary contrast found an invalid control overlap: the most recent ordinary signal could be immediately before the extreme, making its forward outcome include the extreme signal interval. This conditions the control outcome on the event being studied. Those matched contrasts are invalid and must not be presented as evidence. Preserve the original artifact as `quote_side_replication_superseded_overlap.json`.

Before recomputing, add the fixed requirement `control_outcome_end <= extreme_signal_interval_start`, using quote timestamps, not array positions. This excludes controls whose forward interval overlaps the extreme's signal-return interval. Add a synthetic regression case with a preceding ordinary observation and a known extreme that would otherwise share its outcome. No threshold, horizon, cost or event definition changes. Absolute event outcomes are unaffected. This is a disclosed methodology repair after inspecting results, so the corrected contrast is exploratory and does not become independent confirmation.
