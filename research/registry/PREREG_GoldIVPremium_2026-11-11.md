# Gold implied-minus-realized volatility

Exploratory single-instrument transfer inspired by https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0261560617301298 (paper title: Volatility risk premia and future commodity returns; publisher PII S0261560617301298). Use Cboe GVZ daily closes and trailing 21-session annualized standard deviation of log XAUUSD 15:45 New York bid-to-bid returns. Both inputs must be from a prior common completed session. Difference in annualized percentage points proxies the premium; historical realized volatility is an imperfect expected-volatility benchmark. Buy gold from 09:45 to 15:45 New York when the prior observation's premium exceeds its previous 252 common observations' 80th percentile. Require prior observation at most four calendar days old. No inverse arm, stops, leverage optimization or parameter sweep. Fixed 0.02 lots, spread, 20-point adverse slippage each side, ECN commission. Compare mean net P&L with long-only random dates drawn within year with equal counts. Test 2011–2015, 2016–2020, 2021–2025. Existing PF gates apply. Historical screen only.
