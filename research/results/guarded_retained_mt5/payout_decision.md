# XAUUSD payout decision under Atlas Access

**Deployment decision: no funded deployment.** Keep Asian Range Filtered plus London Momentum Tight as the smallest forward-demo candidate. Retain the three-strategy version as a research comparator for payout timing and cost sensitivity. Both earned the same 4 modeled withdrawals across 8 fresh quarterly starts. Their mean cash after fees at cutoff was negative. No standalone strategy qualified, and no Luna version earned inclusion. Actual payments were $0.

The phased portfolio uses the original three-strategy evaluation book at 0.02 lots. On passing, it starts a fresh $5,000 funded account, trades 0.01 lots, permits one position at a time, attaches a stop at entry, rejects planned loss above $30 plus a $5 reserve, and attempts to exit at $60 floating loss. New York Session Reversal is excluded. Each annual test starts from January 1. The 2025 window ends December 31 and the 2026 window ends October 31. All dates below are UTC.

## What changes the result

Eight fresh native January, April, July and October evaluation starts provide a stronger timing check than rebasing fills. Seven reached funding and four produced one modeled $100 payout before their respective year cutoffs. Three funded accounts were still active without a payout and one was still in evaluation. Zero breaches were observed across these eight attempts. For a start chosen with equal weight from these dates, modeled cash at cutoff averaged **−$23.25 per attempt**, calculated from $400 of receipts, seven $83 activated attempts and one $5 entry-only attempt. These overlapping scenarios are alternatives. The average is neither an independent success probability nor a lifetime loss estimate. [Fresh native attempt records](report.md)

Frozen historical fills were replayed from each monthly start with a fresh balance and tick-level rule checks. This is a sensitivity to timing. The start months overlap, later attempts have less time, and the replay keeps the subsequent native fills fixed. Several starts can share the very same payout. The arithmetic mean treats each start as a separate alternative and is descriptive only.

## Strategy evidence

Removing Asian Breakout leaves Asian Range Filtered and London Momentum Tight. The two-strategy account passed its January-start evaluation on April 28, 2025 and March 19, 2026. It funded on April 29 and March 20. The 2025 funded run earned $350.76, PF 1.77, with 37 wins from 80 trades and a $100 modeled payout October 28. The 2026 funded run earned $317.05, PF 1.59, with 29 wins from 59 trades and a $100 modeled payout October 19. Both survived to cutoff. Adding Asian Breakout advanced the 2026 payout by 20 days and preserved that payout under $0.50 of extra round-trip cost, where the two-strategy account lost eligibility. Both variants lost payout eligibility under the $1 stress in both years. None of the three strategies qualified alone in both years. Using the phased policy in 2025, Asian Range Filtered earned $191.79 funded, Asian Breakout $256.48 and London $153.18. Only Breakout earned a modeled $100 payout. In 2026 their respective full-window funded trading results were $118.90, $174.54 and $230.09. Both Asian accounts breached through inactivity and London remained active with zero payouts. Full-window trading P&L includes trading beyond an account's breach. Under a 0.01-lot guard in both phases, the three-strategy book remained active after passing in September 2026. Four funded days earned at least $25 each. ## Next decision

The [research roster](payout_roster.json) records an empty deployment list. For a forward demo, lock the two-strategy settings before observing new dates, record all signals, costs and equity in sequence, then follow a predefined evaluation start through funding and the next eligible withdrawal. Record elapsed time and every active, unpaid or breached attempt at the same horizon. The purchase decision needs a positive after-fee result across starts under broker-specific conditions, PF of at least 1.3 after costs and zero observed breaches. Historical January starts alone do not establish that result.
