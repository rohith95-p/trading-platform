# Forecasting the next 15 minutes of gold

Does the last hour of completed M15 gold returns improve next-bar forecasts over a constant? This is a bounded market-behavior study requested in the original brief. No trading strategy, fills, optimization or payout simulation.

Use load_master M15 closes ending before November 2026. Keep only windows with six finite positive closes exactly 900 seconds apart (four input returns followed by one target return). No windows cross closures. Fit exactly two OLS models with an intercept to targets in 2004–2018 UTC. One predicts signed log return in bps from its four lags, the other predicts absolute log return in bps from its four absolute lags. Coefficients and training-mean baselines freeze across all evaluation eras. Show the four-lag coefficient as the marginal lag-four linear dependence, without assigning causal meaning. No clipping or parameter search.

Evaluate 2019–2024, 2025 and available 2026 through October separately. Report counts and coverage, mean squared error relative to the fixed training mean, signed-direction hit rate and the same score for the constant direction, and realized signed move following forecast sign in USD/oz. That last statistic is close-to-close before costs. Report month-cluster bootstrap 95% intervals on mean paired squared-error gain vs baseline (1999 draws, fixed seed 20261118). One pair of fixed models and three descriptive evaluation windows. For a broad improvement claim require positive relative skill in all three and lower 95% bounds above zero in both 2019–24 and 2025. Directional improvement qualifies only for a later executable-cost study. A magnitude improvement motivates volatility/risk work only. Previously explored market history remains research data. No holdout use and no variants after observing outcomes.
