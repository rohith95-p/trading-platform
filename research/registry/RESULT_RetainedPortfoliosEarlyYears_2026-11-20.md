# Retained XAUUSD portfolios in 2022–2024

Completed 15 native MT5 evaluation runs and two fresh funded runs. The two- and three-strategy portfolios both lost money and missed the evaluation target in 2022 and 2023. Both passed in May 2024, then lost $120.14 and $73.95 respectively while funded. The six portfolio attempts earned zero modeled payouts and incurred $186 in modeled fees. No standalone attempt reached funding or paid.

The quote archive counts matched every calendar evaluation. The 2022 MT5 report displayed 99% real ticks and the 2023 and 2024 reports displayed 100%. The funded and evaluation deals reconcile to native report P&L. No configuration qualified for funded deployment.
