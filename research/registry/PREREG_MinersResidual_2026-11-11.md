# Unexpected gold-miner returns

Exploratory hypothesis: a large miner ETF return unexplained by simultaneous gold ETF and broad equity returns contains information about the following US session in gold. Daily close log returns of GDX, GLD, SPY on common dates. OLS intercept and two slopes on the previous 126 observations only. Act when the current residual exceeds 1.5 times training residual RMS in magnitude. Follow residual direction on the next ETF session at 09:45 NY; close at 15:45. Fixed 0.02 lots, ECN costs, no additional parameter variants. Compare with following the prior GLD return on the same opportunities, and shuffling residual directions within calendar year. Score 2007–2012, 2013–2018, 2019–2025. No advancement unless each era reaches PF 1.30, pooled target 1.40 and positive incremental performance versus controls. Screening only.
