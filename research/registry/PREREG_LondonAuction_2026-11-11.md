Two directions: fade or follow the move. Stop one pre-entry range away; time exit at 16:15. No target or parameter sweep. Require all 91 consecutive minute observations. Historical proxy assumes the cached gold contract. Test 2005–2011, 2012–2018, 2019–2025 separately. Minimum PF 1.30 in every era, target 1.40 pooled, positive lower month-resampled expectancy bound and superiority to a same-opportunity direction control before Atlas lifecycle testing.
