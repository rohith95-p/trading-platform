# Gold ahead of nominal 10-year US Treasury auctions

One new XAUUSD candidate. The market mechanism is temporary dealer inventory pressure before a predictable supply of duration. Lou et al. and the New York Fed's [intraday auction study](https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/staff_reports/sr1188.html) motivate a pre-auction rise in Treasury yields. Whether it is large enough to depress gold is our conjecture. No contemporaneous yield, auction outcome or previous strategy signal is an input.

## Fixed experiment

- Use the [Treasury auction register](https://fiscaldata.treasury.gov/datasets/treasury-securities-auctions-data/) via the Fiscal Data auctions_query endpoint. Require Note, original term 10-Year (including reopenings), nominal fixed rate, competitive close at 1 PM Eastern, announcement dated before auction. Deduplicate by auction day, reject ambiguous rows. Retain schedule fields only.
- On each included date sell XAUUSD at 11:45 America/New_York. Exit at 12:45 Eastern or earlier via 2 ATR stop / 4 ATR target using completed M15 bars and the existing production risk formula. Exit before auction results. No other entries, no other tenors. Fixed 0.02 lots, USD 5000 research balance, one position. Research only. Matched baseline is exactly the same short seven calendar days earlier, same local clock, excluding other eligible 10-year auction dates. No replacement controls. Analyze matched pairs only and log every exclusion.
- From load_master(), require at least 260 prior M15 bars, 16 contiguous completed M15 risk bars directly preceding entry, all M15 slots and M1 minutes through the exit open inclusive. Respect the declared M1 hole. Fail on missed orders or unchecked exits. Use BacktestEngine causal minute chronology and historical spread with 20 points adverse slippage and USD 3.50 round-trip commission per lot. For dates where the archive has no spread the engine uses the scenario fallback. Data availability checks are retrospective limits on measurement.
- Fixed periods 2010–2018, 2019–2024, then 2025 and available Jan–Oct 2026 separately. Nov 2026 onward is sealed. Main gates apply to the first two periods. Report later observations regardless of direction and label sparse coverage. All are historical research periods.
- Continue only if BOTH primary periods have >=30 pairs, PF >=1.30, positive net after deleting the best trade, and the lower bound of each paired advantage over control is positive (calendar-quarter cluster bootstrap, 1999 draws, seed 20261118, two-sided 95% interval). Require positive aggregate later net with at least ten later pairs. Report PF, win rate, net, expectancy, sampled equity drawdown, annual results, and paired uncertainty. PF >=1.40 preferred. A failed gate retires this exact thesis. No parameter changes, reverse trade, new horizon, or alternative matching after seeing results.
- Only on a base pass rerun the candidate with doubled spread and 60 points slippage and perform broader selection-adjusted and account lifecycle research. No Atlas qualification from this screen. Budget is one candidate, one control, at most one conditional stress replay. No subscription or live orders.
