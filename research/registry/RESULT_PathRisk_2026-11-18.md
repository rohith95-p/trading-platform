# Price-path risk on the master XAUUSD M15 series

Of 27,110 complete four-hour blocks, the price reached the upper barrier first in 12,729, the lower first in 13,000, and neither in 211. Both barriers were touched within the same first-touch bar in 1,170 blocks, making the order unidentifiable. The upper and lower barriers were each one prior M15 RMS log return from the opening price. We excluded 6,536 candidate starts with incomplete subsequent M15 grids. Among touched paths, the median first-touch interval was the first 15 minutes. M15 timestamps only bound the touch time.

The largest daily observed-close drawdown in the underlying spot series was 44.84%, with a December 2015 trough and observed recovery in July 2020. The series includes mixed source histories and unobserved intervals. This measures a price decline per ounce. Leveraged strategy drawdowns require marked positions, costs and sizing.

The daily macro proxies showed an average following-row gold return of −2.19 basis points when the dollar and nominal yield both rose, and +9.35 when both fell. Daily market close conventions, non-synchronous pricing, availability times, existing search history and the unresolved anomalies in the futures proxy all limit trading interpretation. No formal significance or promotion criterion was applied to these descriptive groups.
